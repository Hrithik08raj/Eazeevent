How do I export my guest list? expand_more

You can export your guest list by navigating to Settings -> Privacy & Data, and clicking "Download My Account Data".

Can I change my dashboard currency? expand_more

Yes, go to Settings -> Application Preferences to change your default currency symbol.

How does AI Vendor Matchmaking work? expand_more

Our AI analyzes your total budget, remaining funds, event date, and specific preferences to suggest the best-rated vendors available in your area.
