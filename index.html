About Eazeevent

Redefining modern event planning

Our Mission

At Eazeevent, our mission is to eliminate the stress and uncertainty of planning any event—from intimate birthdays and religious pujas to grand weddings and corporate product launches. We empower hosts with AI-driven tools, real-time availability, and a curated selection of India's finest, 100% verified vendors.

Our Story

Founded with the vision of bringing transparency to the unorganized event sector, Eazeevent bridges the gap between premium service providers and customers. We introduced industry-first features like Escrow Protection and Backup Guarantees to ensure every celebration goes off without a hitch.

Why Choose Us?

  • Trust & Security: Escrow payments mean your money is safe until the service is delivered.
  • AI-Powered Insights: Predictive heat-maps for venue availability and intelligent budget tracking.
  • Quality Assured: Every vendor undergoes a strict background and legal verification process.
policy

Booking & Cancellation Policy

Secure terms for both hosts and vendors

event_seat 1. Securing Your Booking

  • A minimum advance payment of 25% of the total contract value is required to lock in your vendor and date.
  • All payments must be routed through the Eazeevent platform to qualify for our Escrow Protection and Backup Guarantee.
  • Once the advance is paid, a legally binding digital contract is automatically generated between the customer and the vendor.

shield 2. Escrow Protection

  • To protect both parties, funds paid by the customer are held securely in an Eazeevent Escrow account.
  • Vendors receive their advance payout 48 hours after the booking is confirmed.
  • The final balance is released to the vendor only after the successful completion of the event.

free_cancellation 3. Customer Cancellations

If a customer decides to cancel an event, the following refund structure applies to the advance paid:

60+ Days before event: 100% Refund
30 to 59 Days before event: 50% Refund
Less than 30 Days before event: No Refund

emergency_home 4. Vendor Cancellations & Backup Guarantee

  • If a vendor cancels, the customer will receive an immediate 100% full refund.
  • The Eazeevent Backup Guarantee: Our concierge team will immediately activate to find a replacement vendor of equal or higher quality at no additional cost to the customer.
  • Vendors who cancel confirmed bookings without a valid force majeure reason will face a penalty and potential suspension from the platform.
